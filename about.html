Our Story

The Journey of Rivaaz

Rivaaz was born from a simple yet powerful idea — to make authentic Indian ethnic wear accessible to every woman who values tradition, elegance and self-expression. What started as a passion for timeless fashion in Surat has now grown into a trusted wholesale destination for boutique owners and retailers who love to embrace cultural roots with a modern touch.

Each piece at Rivaaz is carefully curated, designed and crafted by master artisans to bring out the best in you — because you deserve nothing but the finest quality fabrics and stitching.
